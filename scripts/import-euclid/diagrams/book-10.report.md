No diagram fabricated.

## QA pass (2026-09-23)

All 119 images were composited onto white and viewed individually (or a few per contact sheet) with the Read tool. 33 were rejected on first view — nearly all for including a strip of the surrounding Latin body text or a running head inside the crop; four (book-10-prop-108/109/110/111) additionally showed faint mirrored ghost-text bleeding through from the reverse side of the leaf across the whole crop, not just at one edge. Every rejected image was re-cropped and re-verified against a fresh preview before being accepted; none were redrawn or had ink fabricated — all fixes only narrow/widen the source crop box or, for the four ghost-text cases, re-run the crop from the cached leaf JPEG with a stricter ink/paper split (see below). `book-10.ts` and the matching `partial-book-10-{A,B}.json` entries were updated with the new pixel dimensions for every image below; `check-book-10.ts` passes (119/119 OK).
