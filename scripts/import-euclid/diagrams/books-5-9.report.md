## QA pass (2026-09-23)

Every one of the 136 PNGs above was composited onto white (`sharp().flatten({background:'#fff'})`) and viewed with the Read tool. A large fraction of Books VII-IX (the "number-line" style diagrams: horizontal/vertical labelled segments) had been cropped with too-generous a bounding box in the original pass, so the box's own ink-trim pulled in whole lines of the surrounding Latin proof text along with the diagram. All rejects were re-cropped from the cached leaf named in the map's own `source` field (`scripts/import-euclid/raw/heiberg-vol2/leaf<N>.jpg`), using a hand-picked tighter region per image (same ink/paper threshold as the original `crop.mjs`, plus a small connected-component despeckle pass to drop isolated paper-dust dots) and re-verified visually after every re-crop; each re-crop's new width/height is reflected in `DIAGRAMS_BOOKS_5_9` above.

**Re-cropped for body-text bleed** (~85 images): all of Book VI props 6, 7-a, 7-b, 8, 10, 12, 13, 19, 20, 21, 22-a, 22-b, 31; nearly all of Book VII (props 1-5, 9-10, 12-14, 16-25, 27-39 - the only Book VII images that needed no rework were 6, 7, 8, 11, 15, 26, 36-b); most of Book VIII (props 1, 3, 4-b, 6, 7, 9-19); and most of Book IX (props 1, 2, 4-18, 20-23, 25, 27-31, 36-a). Book IV.16 (light paper speckle only, no text) was despeckled the same way.

**Accepted with caveat** - re-cropped as tightly as the source allows but a small amount of adjacent print could not be fully excluded without cutting the figure itself:
- `book-6-prop-22-b.png`: a small marginal square figure sitting hard against the gutter; a faint vertical bleed-through/gutter-shadow band remains on the left edge.
- `book-7-prop-31.png`: the topmost segment in this figure has no letter label visible on the printed page at all next to it in either honest crop attempt (only the tick mark) - not a cropping artifact, the label is simply implied by the running text rather than printed beside the line.
- `book-7-prop-39.png`, `book-8-prop-15.png`, `book-8-prop-16.png`, `book-9-prop-5.png`, `book-9-prop-6.png`, `book-9-prop-30.png`: the diagram's own line(s) sit close enough to the neighbouring proof-text column that a sliver of a header word or a line-end remains in the tightest honest crop; the diagram's own ink is complete.
