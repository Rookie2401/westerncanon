# The course — a reading path through the original-language edition

*Contract for the course build (2026-10-07). Built on the Lexis language help
(`docs/LEXIS-PLAN.md`) and modelled on Go's ladder, Stage 0 and GoLearn
evidence model (`go-v0/docs/LADDER.md`, `docs/STAGE0.md`, `src/golearn/*`).
Everything here is gated exactly like Lexis: `EDITION !== 'en'` and the
"Language help" toggle. The English edition never sees any of it.*

## 1. What the reader gets

- **Your path** (`#/path`): one ordered reading ladder per language (Greek,
  Latin, Italian) through the library's own texts, computed from the texts'
  vocabulary: each unit is placed where the texts before it have already
  shown the reader most of its words. Stage 0 comes first; then stages
  I–V, bands of the growing vocabulary pool. Each unit says how long it is,
  how much of it the path has already taught (curriculum coverage), and —
  for the units just ahead — how much of it *this* reader is estimated to
  know (a labelled estimate, never a score).
- **Stage 0** (`#/path/:lang/start`): the on-ramp. The script (Greek
  alphabet, breathings, accents, iota subscript; Latin and Italian letters
  and sounds), pronunciation conventions named honestly, decoding drills on
  real words from the corpus, the first 300 words by corpus frequency with
  their dictionary glosses, and micro-passages: real sentences from the
  library whose every word is among those first words, cited to their work
  and section, glossed word by word. Nothing in Stage 0 is written for the
  course: every sentence is verbatim from a text in the library. There are
  no translations of the micro-passages because none exist in the corpus
  for a sentence in isolation; the glosses are from the dictionaries.
- **Evidence** (GoLearn's five notions, never merged):
  - *displayed* — opening a section counts for nothing;
  - *seen* — a section the reader dwelt on (≥ 5 s; the Lexis read log);
  - *finished* — only an explicit "Finished this unit" (never inferred);
  - *understood* — only the reader's own report (all / most / some / little);
  - *estimated familiarity* — per lemma, from encounters, lookups and
    sittings, always labelled "estimate".
- **Readiness** (`#/progress`): advice about moving past the current stage,
  from several signals (lookups per 100 words, English rescues per section,
  reported understanding, retention after a week, how the next units would
  read). A verdict is `insufficient data`, `stay` or `ready to advance`.
  It never locks anything: every text in the library stays open.
- **Help that fades by stage**: the stage of the unit being read chooses a
  default help profile (highlighting, morphology on first tap). The reader
  can switch "Help follows your path" off in Settings and choose manually.
- **English rescue**: when the English edition has the same section (same
  division id in the sibling work), the reader gets a link to it; opening
  it is recorded as a rescue, like Go's `EnglishRescueRevealed`.
- **Honesty**: the path is an order computed from vocabulary statistics,
  nothing more. No text is simplified, re-ordered inside itself, or written.
  The first units of Greek and Latin are hard — the library has an ocean and
  no beach — and the path says so rather than pretending otherwise.

### 2.1 Units (chunks) and candidates (sections)

A **unit** is what the reader is asked to read next: one or more consecutive
reader stops (the divisions the reader's Prev/Next walks: leaf divisions, or a
consolidated group such as Euclid's Definitions) of one work, grouped to
roughly 800–2 500 running words (a single stop longer than that is its own
unit; Iliad books are ~4–6 k words each). A unit never crosses a top-level
division (Book, Act, Canto) unless the top-level divisions are themselves
tiny (drama cards of ~50 words), in which case consecutive cards are grouped.

A **section** is the candidate the ladder algorithm places: the run of units
sharing one top-level division (a Book), or a single grouped unit where the
top-level divisions are tiny. Placing a section appends its units in order,
so a Book is never interleaved with other works, and the Books of one work
stay in their own order.

### 2.2 Ordering (deterministic)

Greedy, with curriculum coverage as in Go's ladder: a lemma counts as
**known** once it has appeared ≥ 3 times in the units placed before; names
(`pos: name`) always count as known; an unrecognised token never does and
stays in the denominator. At each step the candidates are the next unplaced
section of every work; the one with the highest coverage wins (ties: fewer
new lemmas per 100 words, then work id, then position). The chosen section's
units are appended and the lemma counts updated. Every unit records the
coverage at its own position (not its section's): those are the numbers the
reader sees.

The algorithm keeps a per-candidate known-token count that is updated
incrementally (only lemmas that just crossed the threshold change it), so
the whole corpus orders in seconds and the result is reproducible from the
committed Lexis bundles.

### 2.3 Stages

| stage | pool of lemmas met ≥ 3 times, after the unit | role |
|---|---|---|
| `0` | — | script, first words, micro-passages (on-ramp) |
| `I` | < 1 000 | first readings: expect to look most words up |
| `II` | < 2 500 | |
| `III` | < 5 000 | |
| `IV` | < 10 000 | |
| `V` | ≥ 10 000 | the library is yours |

### 2.4 Assistance profiles (defaults while "Help follows your path" is on)

| stage | highlight | morphology on first tap | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0, I | all | on | every word marked, full help on first tap |
| II | new | on | only new words marked |
| III | new | off | |
| IV, V | none | off | help on request only |

English (the rescue link) is always reachable, at every stage.

## 3. Evidence (runtime, `src/course/evidence.ts`)

Dexie `westerncanon-lexis` version 2 adds one append-only table:
`events: ++id, type, lang, workId, at`. Lexis' `reads` (exposure) and
`lookups` tables are the other two evidence sources; `reads` rows gain an
optional `words` count so lookup rates can be computed from the log alone.

| event | meaning |
|---|---|
| `UnitCompleted { unitId, lang }` | explicit completion |
| `ComprehensionReported { unitId, lang, degree }` | all / most / some / little |
| `RescueOpened { workId, divId, lang }` | the English section was opened |
| `Stage0StepCompleted { lang, step }` | a Stage 0 section was marked done |
| `Stage0DecodeAnswered { lang, item, correct, hinted }` | a decoding drill answer |
| `Stage0MatchAnswered { lang, item, correct }` | a first-words meaning drill answer |

Familiarity per lemma (`familiarityOf`): `known` (status known / mastered /
ignored), `likely-familiar` (≥ 4 encounters on ≥ 2 days and no lookup since
the last two encounters), `met` (any encounter or lookup), `new`.

Predicted coverage of a unit for this reader = share of its running tokens
whose lemma is known or likely-familiar, plus half of those merely met.

## 4. Readiness (`src/course/readiness.ts`)

Signals over the reader's evidence in the language (thresholds in
`src/course/policy.ts`, provisional):

| signal | data | met when |
|---|---|---|
| lookup-rate | lookups per 100 words over the last 30 seen sections | ≤ 4 |
| rescue-rate | English rescues per 100 seen sections (same window) | ≤ 5 |
| reported-comprehension | share of the last reports saying all/most (≥ 3) | ≥ 0.8 |
| retention | lemmas first met ≥ 7 days ago and met again in the last 7, share without a lookup in those 7 days (≥ 30) | ≥ 0.85 |
| unfamiliar-material | predicted coverage of the next 3 unopened units of the path | ≥ 0.90 |
| decoding (Stage 0 only) | last 10 decode answers correct without the hint | ≥ 8 |

Verdict: `insufficient-data` with fewer than 10 sections seen in the stage or
fewer than 3 signals with data; `ready-to-advance` when every signal with
data is met; otherwise `stay`. Finishing every unit of a stage is not a
signal. Advice, never a gate.

## 6. Acceptance

- `npm run typecheck`, `lint`, `test` green; `npm run build` builds both
  editions; the English edition ships no `course/` directory.
- `npm run course:check` reports every unit's coverage, cliffs and new-lemma
  rates; the committed ladders are re-checked by a test.
- The rendered text with the course on is character-identical to the text
  with it off (nothing is inserted into a passage; the path bar sits after
  the passages).

- The first unit of each language is the one the 300 first words cover best
  (Greek: Aristotle's *Prior Analytics* at 85 %; Latin: Boethius' *De
  Hebdomadibus* at 81 %). Lexically easiest is not conceptually easiest; the
  Path page says so. The same-author rule (`SAME_AUTHOR_PENALTY`) interleaves
  Porphyry's Isagoge into the Aristotle run; it is deliberately mild.
- Stage V's lowest-coverage units are the fragmentary and technical texts
  (Sophocles' *Ichneutae* with 15–20 % of tokens unrecognised by the analyser,
  the Almagest, Euclid's lettered propositions): the "unrecognised" share is
  printed on their rows.
- Nothing is reviewed by a person: the script tables are authored content
  (sources named on the page); the first words and micro-passages are
  mechanical selections from the corpus and the dictionaries.
- Not built: audio, listening, production (GoTell), controlled or adapted
  texts — this library has none and the course writes none.
